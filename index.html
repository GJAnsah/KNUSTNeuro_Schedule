Overview & Learning Outcomes

The Neuromuscular Short Course is a virtual program introducing rising senior biomedical engineering undergrada to the fundamentals and applications of neural engineering and biomechanics. Over one week of instruction and one week of guided project work, students learn how neural signals are generated, recorded, and translated into technologies that restore movement and sensation.

What you'll learn

  • Neural signals and intracortical BCIs
  • Neural data recording and analysis (EEG, fNIRS)
  • Neuromechanical systems, motion capture, and EMG
  • Applied translation and clinical context
  • Independent project design and presentation

Who it's for

Rising senior engineering undergraduates at KNUST with an interest in neuroscience, biomedical engineering, or bioinstrumentation.

Prerequisites

Basic Python/MATLAB programming.
